### L3 SOURCE NORMALIZATION

SOURCE PATTERN:
- someone initiates lunch selection;
- nobody wants responsibility for the decision;
- "anything is fine" is not actually true;
- concrete suggestions reveal hidden preferences;
- time gets wasted;

VOICE TO PRESERVE:

- nobody is the villain;
- humor comes from group behavior being predictably irrational;
- the ending should feel "아 이거 맨날 이러는데" rather than a scripted joke.

KOREAN LOCALIZATION:
- ordinary office team around 11:50–12:00;
- realistic lunch candidates such as 제육 / 국밥 / 돈까스 / 냉면 / 김치찌개;
- final destination should be a generic familiar Korean lunch place, not a real branded restaurant.

### L4 STORY SHAPE
DECISION_LOOP → REJECTION CASCADE → FAMILIAR DEFAULT.

### L5 STORY BEATS — draft

1. HOOK — 11:52. Someone says, "오늘 점심 뭐 먹지?"
2. OPENNESS — everyone answers variations of "난 아무거나."
3. FIRST SUGGESTION — one person proposes a specific menu.
4. REJECTION CASCADE — suddenly each person has a reason that one does not work; two or three alternatives also die quickly.
5. TIME CHECK — they realize they have spent way too long deciding and are now actually hungry / late.
6. PAYOFF — cut to the group sitting at the same familiar lunch place again.
7. TAG — one person casually says something equivalent to, "여기가 제일 무난하긴 해."

Recommended final length: 6 slides.
Beat 3-4 can be compressed into one slide if visual pacing is stronger at 5 slides.
