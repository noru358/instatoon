# E001 STORY SOURCE PACK

Episode: E001  
Working label: 편의점 음유시인  
Source mode: community anecdote / secondary report  
Verification: **unverified internet anecdote**

## Source

A 2016 국민일보 article summarized an online-community post written from the perspective of a convenience-store worker.

## Source truth

- A convenience-store worker described a male regular who often came in after drinking and was normally considered troublesome.
- On the relevant day, the worker continued to respond to him kindly.
- The customer unexpectedly became serious and told the worker: **“너는… 니가 살고 싶은 세상에서 살아.”**
- He then opened two ice bars and put them into the worker’s two hands.
- He insisted that the worker take a bite before he would leave.
- The worker ultimately took a bite; the shared image reportedly showed both ice bars in hand, with a bite missing from one.

## Concrete details to preserve

1. ordinary convenience-store counter;
2. drunken troublesome regular;
3. unusually patient/kind response by worker;
4. sudden tonal shift into a serious sentence;
5. **two** ice bars, already opened;
6. one ice bar in each hand;
7. he does not leave until one bite is taken;
8. final visual: worker left holding two ice bars, one bitten.

## Emotional turns

`fatigue → cautious kindness → confusion → unexpected warmth → renewed confusion → absurd aftertaste`

- do not add romance/family tragedy;
- do not invent a bigger punchline;
- do not identify a store or person.
