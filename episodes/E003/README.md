## 3. Story premise

On the way to a restroom, Gaeun meets a friendly stranger.
The stranger compliments her outfit, then points out her untied shoelace.
Gaeun reflexively apologizes for having an untied shoelace.
She realizes the weirdness, then immediately makes things worse by telling the stranger "see you later" as both enter the same restroom.
Inside the stall, she silently cringes at herself.
