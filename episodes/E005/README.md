## 4. Seven-beat story — L10 PASS

01 — HOOK / FIRST DATE
Three-year friends are on their first real date and it is going unexpectedly well.
Later lettering:
"3년 알고 지낸 남사친이랑 / 처음 데이트한 날."

02 — GOODBYE
He walks her to the apartment door; they say an easy, slightly shy goodbye.

03 — IMPULSE
The door closes. Keys are still in her hand. She turns around, smiles, and immediately sends the marriage-prediction message to what she thinks is her friend.

04 — REVEAL
She sees the recipient and realizes she sent it to the man from the date.

05 — DAMAGE CONTROL
She drops/crouches to the entryway floor and fires off the explanation that it was meant for her friend.

06 — REPLY
His reply arrives: he will treat it as unread. Her embarrassment peaks, then slightly releases.

07 — AFTERSHOCK / FOUR-YEAR UPDATE
Four-year-later update card: the same couple, now married.
Precise lettering direction:
"4년 뒤 근황"
"진짜 결혼함."

No moral and no invented extra punchline.
